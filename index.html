Body Decoded Studio

Body Decoded Studio 是 YouTube 頻道「身體解碼」自用的影片製作與上傳工具，只用於把頻道自己製作的影片上傳到本頻道，並設定標題、說明、標籤、縮圖與發布時間。

Body Decoded Studio is a private tool used only by the YouTube channel "身體解碼" to upload its own videos and manage their metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail and publish time).
